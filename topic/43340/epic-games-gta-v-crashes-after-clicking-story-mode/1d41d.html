        @JonSnowFS said in Epic Games GTA V crashes after clicking story mode:

        So I tried reinstalling the games 2-3 times in the gap of few months but still the same issue

        Why would reinstalling more than once make any difference? Unless you have a defective drive or defective system memory all it takes is one installation and you're good for the rest of your game's life. If you did get an improper installation and/or a corrupt file you'll very likely get an error message or warning to that effect. One installation is all it takes.

        Why do some people say they fixed their game after reinstalling? Because they installed mods directly to the game and screwed it up. I probably have over 100 games installed, about 15 of these are modded, my GTA V is modded beyond limits - none of these games have been reinstalled. They crash, you find the cause, you fix it.

        ( I even transferred steam gta v files into epic gta v folder but same issue )

        Very bad idea. You don't mix the games from different platforms.

        So today after 2+ years I installed the game again by transferring files from steam to epic but after 2+ years I am facing the same issue. Game crashes after clicking on story mode after loading few seconds.

        Even worse. Once more you're introducing all kinds of unwarranted variables instead of simplifying.

        I even freshly installed windows recently but same issue.

        That's kinda like installing a new engine in the car because it won't start and the real reason is your battery is dead or the gas tank is empty. The only reason to reinstall windows is when you want a "fresh copy", not to fix a game.

        Playing mods, non-cheating mods, can't get you banned on GTAO because SH5 and OpenIV are both designed not to let that happen.

        I have no idea what you did to your game, but given your bad luck I would do a verify integrity with the Steam version and then follow this guide:

        https://gta5modhelp.blogspot.com/p/troubleshooting_4.html
