      @ManOfMystery It can be done easily if scripthook has get moon phase native, or at least has a function to call game day and differentiate the days between each weeks. I think there are 3 weeks in game until Moon phase starts from the beginning. Try ChatGPT, it might help you with it. Not an expert but doesn't sound too complicated.

      By the by, there is no Moon tide in game. The sea level rising up is made with a simple trick in weather.xml called WaveAmplitude. It's values higher in Rainy, Thunder and Clearing weathers compared to others. So when it rains, sea levels rise up. Although it is realistic, it has nothing to do with Moon tides.

      Moon tides occur by the phase and position of the Moon itself, not by the weather types.
