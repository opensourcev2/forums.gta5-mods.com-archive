      @NJ_Get_Lit Your problem is unusual, infrequent, and OpenIV definitely doesn't require execution with admin privileges, nor does it require setting any specific permissions. It is installed like most Windows software. So unless you don't have any permissions, other than read only, on your own PC, I would look elsewhere.

      I've seen this once before where the folder itself was corrupt in some way, impacting permissions. So that could possibly be the solution, create a new folder and dump your files in...without reinstalling. I'm assuming you downloaded OpenIV from the legit site.

      Edit. Or just create a folder on your desktop or elsewhere, copy gta5.exe to that folder and point to that folder when OpenIV prompts you for the location. If OpenIV accepts the folder, just terminate the installation. Then you'll now the game folder is your issue.

      @denqxd keep in mind that Rockstar, and game studios in general, optimize their models for memory considerations. This is also why cars don't persist and why there are LODs.

      Mod vehicles are rarely optimized so replacing many vanilla cars with high polygon counts and 4k textures will lead to instability.
