      • PatrickSLC

      D

      oh yes the old mod kit issue... to get on top of this problem you need to go in and make mod kits for every car you personally add on ... if two cars use 165... they will have absolutely no mods available to either car.
      i made a document where i record every car i add into the game. and i reassign the mod list ... thats just so i can keep track.
      (assuming you already know how to add cars properly. via open IV and have properly set up a mods folder.

      step 1 located in the mods folder your dlc file.
      should be.... OpenIV/GTA5/Mods/update/x64/dlcpacks

      find your car by model name. my example will use the count5 (lamborghini countach)

      step 2 go to the data file for the dlc addon car
      should be example... OpenIV/GTA5/Mods/update/x64/dlcpacks/count5/data
      (some addon cars the author has set the files up differently your objective is to find two files)
      carcols.meta
      carvariations.meta

      step 3 carcols.meta...
      the target line will be near the top may be 5th line or in my case its the 7th line
      <kitName>888_count5_modkit</kitName>
      (this is the original number assigned by the mod author)

      if this were the problematic car you can neither mod this car in game or what ever other car its conflicting.

      original cars use the early 100 - 200s so i tend to go way and above usually above 1000...

      general rule of thumb you can use any number from 500 to 999999999999999999 infinity. but i generally
      try to stay within the hundred thousands. 888111 or something of such as i will never have 100,000 addons lol.

      unfortunately you would have to find the other car by searching for it in openiv. and mod authors have no way in knowing what other numbers have been used by other authors... frankly its not their problem... they made one addon car and their only worry should be not to conflict with other addons they have released...

      so in carcols.meta these two line exist and literally any number can be used as long as its not ever reused.
      <kitName>888_count5_modkit</kitName>
      <id value="888" />

      change to
      <kitName>8881_count5_modkit</kitName>
      <id value="8881" />

      after making the change to these two lines make sure you save the file and go to the next file.

      step 4 carvariations.meta
      open this file and search modkit in the search bar... should be close to the bottom of the file.
      in my case it is line 220
      <Item>888_count5_modkit</Item>
      the line should match what ever you change you made in the other file in my case i added a 1 to the end of the number.
      <Item>8881_count5_modkit</Item>

      save and close the file.

      then you can close openiv

      the car should have working mods again.
      if you plan to use several add on cars it is good practice everytime you add a car
      to change these numbers anyway that way you never get a conflict...

      an important note to take
      if the line reads as follows
      <kitName>0_default_modkit</kitName>
      then this vehicle was design with no moddable parts thus you dont need to change the number.
